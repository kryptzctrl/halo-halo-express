DIFFICULTY
GAME
Every order needs shaved ice + evap milk plus the exact toppings in the bubble.
• Drag items into any glass (or click to add to the selected one). Click a topping layer to remove it.
• Serve before the patience bar empties. 3 angry customers = game over.
• Correct orders in a row build a combo bonus; fast service earns tips.
• ⚡ RUSH customers are hasty but pay more; 👑 VIP customers pay a lot.
• Mix-Mix Spoon: drag circles over a glass to stir it for a +₱15 bonus.
• Special recipes (named orders) need one topping added last. Serve them to discover them in the Recipe Book.
• Waiting line: from Day 3 customers queue up. Click a waiting customer to serve them first, but everyone loses patience.
• Snacks: some orders add Turon, Banana Cue or Buko Juice. Tap the right snack on the snack plate before serving.
• Events (heat wave, power outage, fiesta, school group) shake things up.
• Earnings go to your Wallet; spend it in the Stall Shop after each day.
• Customers differ: Lola is patient, students are hasty, tourists pay more.
• Freeze Time pauses patience. Each level-up lets you pick an upgrade.
• Keys: 1-9 glass, Enter serve, I ice, M milk, F freeze, Backspace trash, P pause.
• Crowds: higher days bring more customers at once, with bigger orders and less patience. Tap any waiting customer to serve them first.
